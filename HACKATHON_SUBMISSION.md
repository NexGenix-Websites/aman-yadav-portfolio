## What I built

I’m Aman Yadav, a senior secondary student preparing for JEE. I built this portfolio to introduce myself, show what I’m currently learning, and take my first steps into cloud hosting. The site has About, Tech Superpowers, and Projects & Achievements sections, with familiar navigation and a responsive layout.

Its creative feature is **Cloud Break**, a short request-routing puzzle. Visitors follow a request from the browser to its hosting service and then to an edge location. A visible hint in the hero invites visitors to try it, and the result reveals a note about my current mix of JEE preparation and cloud curiosity. It sits inside a dialog so the main navigation remains easy to use.
